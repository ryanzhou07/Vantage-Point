# VP-026 — 30-day split link (long-lived participant access)

## Status
`BACKLOG`

## Owner
Receipt Agent (Integration/Review separate)

## Purpose
Split sessions currently last 1–60 minutes (default 5; `SPLIT_SESSION_TTL`, enforced in `session.go`). The owner wants the share link to stay valid for 30 days so participants can come back, change their claims and payment method, and see what they owe. A link that lives 30 days is a long-lived bearer credential, so the security model needs deliberate changes rather than just a larger number.

## Scope
- Raise the maximum session lifetime to 30 days and make 30 days the default for owner-created share links. Keep the configurable bounds (new range 1 minute to 30 days) so tests and local runs can still use short values.
- Define what stays editable and for how long (see Requirements 2–3).
- Owner controls: a visible "link expires on <date>" and an existing-style "invalidate link" action that takes effect immediately, plus "create a new link".
- Participant experience on return: the same link restores their identity, claims and payment method (VP-021) as long as the receipt is not finalized.

## Out of Scope
Making participants canonical users or adding accounts for them, email or SMS delivery of links, push notifications, changing the public payload-free Realtime channel decision, payee handles.

## Dependencies
- VP-015a/b/c (sessions, join, claims), VP-021 (payment method).
- Memory/owner decision: a session may start only after the receipt is confirmed.

## Requirements
1. The raw token is never stored; only its SHA-256 hash (already true) and it is never logged. A 30-day lifetime makes this stricter, not looser.
2. Editing is allowed while the receipt is not finalized. At finalization the session becomes read-only: participants can still open the link to see their amount owed and chosen method, but cannot change claims or method.
3. After finalization the link stays valid for viewing until it expires or the owner invalidates it. It must expose only that participant's own data: their name, share, amount owed, status and method. No other participants' data, no owner contact data, no receipt location.
4. The owner can invalidate a link at any time; invalidation applies to the very next request.
5. Per-token and per-IP rate limits remain and are tuned for repeat visits (return traffic must not trip the limiter, while enumeration stays blocked).
6. Tokens carry at least 128 bits of entropy and are compared by hash lookup; verify the existing generator.
7. Expired and invalidated links return the same non-revealing response.
8. Participant name changes and claims remain atomic and concurrency-safe as in VP-015c.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] A new share link reports an expiry about 30 days out; `SPLIT_SESSION_TTL` outside 1m–30d is rejected.
- [ ] A participant reopens the link after a refresh and sees their claims and method intact.
- [ ] After finalization the same link shows read-only amount owed and rejects every mutation.
- [ ] Invalidating the link blocks the next request from any device.
- [ ] A forged, expired, invalidated or other receipt's token yields an identical response (no existence leak).
- [ ] A finalized-view response contains no other participant's data (test).

## Testing Requirements
Go: TTL parsing bounds, expiry and invalidation, post-finalization read-only, response-shape leak tests, rate limiting for repeat visits. SQL: token hash constraint and expiry. Frontend: returning-participant view and read-only state. Targeted runs only.

## Shared Resource Impact
Shared contracts: YES (`agents/contracts/RECEIPT_SESSIONS.md` expiry semantics and the post-finalization view). Authentication: NO (participants stay non-users).

## Database Impact
`NEW MIGRATION` only if the DB enforces a maximum lifetime or needs a read-only/finalized transition column. Do not edit applied migrations.

## Contract Impact
`NON-BREAKING CHANGE` for create/join; `NEW CONTRACT` for the post-finalization participant view.

## Security Considerations
Main risk: a leaked 30-day link grants access for a month. Mitigations are hashed storage, immediate owner invalidation, a minimal read-only data surface after finalization, rate limits, and no sensitive fields. Consider whether the link should be re-issuable per participant so one leaked link does not expose everyone's session; record the decision in the implementation notes or an ADR.

## Financial Correctness Considerations
Claims and allocations must stay transactional. A returning participant must never alter finalized amounts.

## Blockers
None. Owner decision (2026-10-29): hybrid links. A shared link/QR is used during the live split. At finalization each participant who owes money receives a personal link (30 days) for viewing their own amount owed, status and payment method. Participants see only their own data.

Cost requirements added by this decision:
- The personal read-only view must not open a Realtime subscription; it is a plain fetch (no open connections for 30 days).
- Personal tokens are created lazily when the owner asks to share them, not for every participant at finalization.
- Expired and invalidated session/token rows are purged by a bounded scheduled job so storage does not grow without limit.
- One indexed lookup by token hash per view; no per-view fan-out queries.
